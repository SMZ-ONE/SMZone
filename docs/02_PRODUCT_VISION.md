\# 02 - PRODUCT VISION



\## Final Product

A subscribable SaaS where a brand connects its store and social accounts, and SMZone auto-generates ideas, texts and visuals from products, publishes with one click, and reports real revenue: "This post sold 5 units".



\## Core Modules

1\. Social Account Manager

2\. Product Catalog (e-commerce sync)

3\. Content Planner (calendar)

4\. AI Center (Brand Brain)

5\. Analytics \& ROI

6\. CRM (comments/DMs) - Phase 4

7\. Brand Knowledge Base - Phase 3+



\## Brand Brain (Key Differentiator)

AI must know for each brand:

\- Brand tone \& forbidden words

\- Campaigns \& seasonal products

\- Competitors \& target audience

\- Past performance



Goal: When founder says "New product arrived", AI behaves like a teammate who knows the brand, not a generic ChatGPT.



\## 3 Levels

Level 1: Working Product (Laravel+Filament) - NOW

Level 2: Sellable SaaS (team, billing, roles)

Level 3: AI Platform (agents, automation, memory)
